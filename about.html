OUR PURPOSE

Style and responsibility can move together.

VerdantStep is a proposed sustainable footwear start-up focused on fashionable, comfortable everyday trainers. The concept is built around using a combination of recycled and responsibly sourced materials while keeping designs practical and versatile.

We plan to sell directly through our e-commerce website, helping us communicate product information clearly and build relationships with customers.

Our commitments

  • Be clear about the materials used in each product.
  • Seek suppliers who can provide credible information about sourcing and production.
  • Prioritise durable, versatile designs that customers can wear regularly.

VerdantStep is an academic business concept. Specific environmental and ethical claims would only be made after appropriate evidence and supplier checks.
